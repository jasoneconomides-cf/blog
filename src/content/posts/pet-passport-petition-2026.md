**A UK Parliament petition is asking the Government to seek an agreement with the EU so that UK residents can use valid EU pet passports again.** The petition is open, but it has not changed the rules and it does not confirm that a new UK pet passport is available.

As of 29 August 2026, Great Britain residents planning to take a dog, cat or ferret to the EU should still follow the current official guidance. GOV.UK says they should get an Animal Health Certificate for the journey from Great Britain to an EU country.

The live petition, titled “Seek agreement with the EU to accept pet passports for UK-EU travel”, closes on **27 November 2026**. It had **2,902 signatures** when we checked it on 29 August 2026.

**[Read and sign petition 767906 on the official UK Parliament website](https://petition.parliament.uk/petitions/767906)**

Signing is a personal choice. Read the petition in full before deciding whether to support it.

## What is pet passport petition 767906 asking for?

The petition asks the Government to negotiate an agreement with the EU that would allow UK residents who already hold valid EU pet passports to use them for UK-to-EU pet travel without obtaining an Animal Health Certificate for every trip.

Its creator argues that repeated AHC appointments create cost, administration and difficulty for frequent travellers, including dual nationals and people whose pets previously travelled on an EU-issued passport.

This is narrower than introducing an entirely new UK-issued pet passport. The petition specifically focuses on recognition of valid EU pet passports held by UK residents.

That distinction matters because several different proposals are often described online as “bringing pet passports back”:

- allowing Great Britain residents to use existing EU-issued pet passports
- introducing a new multi-use UK pet travel document
- obtaining a different status or wider agreement with the EU
- extending or replacing the current Animal Health Certificate system

These routes might produce a similar practical benefit for some owners, but they are not the same policy.

## What happens if the petition reaches 10,000 signatures?

The UK Parliament petitions service explains that:

- at **10,000 signatures**, the Government will respond to the petition
- at **100,000 signatures**, the petition will be considered for a debate in Parliament

Reaching either threshold would not automatically change the law or the EU entry rules. A government response could accept, reject or qualify the request. Consideration for a debate is also not a guarantee that a debate will take place or that the requested agreement will follow.

The signature total changes as people sign. Check the [official petition page](https://petition.parliament.uk/petitions/767906) for its current progress rather than relying on a figure copied elsewhere.

## Why has this petition appeared now?

New EU pet-travel rules took effect on 22 April 2026. DEFRA and the Animal and Plant Health Agency said that Great Britain residents should no longer use EU pet passports to travel into the EU.

The official announcement says EU pet passports may now only be issued to people whose main home is in the EU. It also says they should not be used by people who merely have a holiday home in the EU or visit seasonally.

That has affected owners who previously obtained an EU pet passport and used it for repeat journeys from Great Britain. Having EU citizenship is not necessarily the same as having your main home in the EU; the official announcement frames the rule around residence.

GOV.UK advises owners resident in Great Britain to get an Animal Health Certificate when travelling from England, Scotland or Wales to an EU country. Read our plain-English explanation of [whether normal vets can issue Animal Health Certificates](/posts/can-normal-vet-issue-animal-health-certificate/) before arranging the appointment.

## Can UK residents use EU pet passports again now?

**Not merely because this petition is open.** A petition records support for a proposed action; it is not itself a change to the travel rules.

The April 2026 GOV.UK announcement says Great Britain residents should no longer use EU pet passports to enter the EU and should obtain an AHC to guarantee smooth travel. It adds that an EU pet passport may still be used for the return journey to Great Britain.

That difference between entering the EU and returning to Great Britain is easy to miss. A document that can be accepted for one direction of the journey is not automatically sufficient for the other.

Individual EU member states can also have their own requirements. Check the official rules for the first EU country you enter and for the final destination, particularly if your journey includes a flight connection or crosses several borders.

## Has the return of a UK pet passport already been agreed?

The UK and EU have said they intend to work towards arrangements that would make pet travel easier, including a multi-use pet passport. However, that does not mean a new passport can currently be obtained, and an exact launch date and final operating rules have not been published.

Our regularly reviewed guide, [Will the UK Pet Passport Return in 2027?](/posts/uk-pet-passport-return-2027/), separates what has been announced from what remains unresolved.

Be cautious with headlines saying UK pet passports “are back” or giving a definite launch date. Before relying on any announcement, look for current GOV.UK instructions explaining:

- when the scheme starts
- who qualifies
- which countries accept the document
- which vets can issue or update it
- whether existing vaccination records can be transferred
- what document owners must use during any transition

Until those details are published, plan a real journey using the rules in force on the travel date.

## What does an Animal Health Certificate allow under the 2026 rules?

An AHC is still a single-trip document for leaving Great Britain for the EU, but its onward validity changed in April 2026.

Current GOV.UK guidance says an AHC is valid for:

- entry into the EU within 10 days after it is issued
- onward travel within the EU for up to six months after entry
- re-entry to Great Britain for up to six months

The pet's rabies vaccination must remain valid. A new AHC is needed for a later trip from Great Britain to the EU.

## What should pet owners do while the petition is open?

If you support the proposal, use the official Parliament link rather than an unofficial copy:

1. Read [petition 767906](https://petition.parliament.uk/petitions/767906) in full.
2. Decide whether you agree with what it specifically asks the Government to do.
3. Complete the official verification process if you choose to sign.
4. Share the official petition accurately, without claiming that it has already changed the rules.
5. Continue using current official travel guidance for any journey you are planning.

For travel in the meantime, check the pet's microchip and rabies records early, confirm the document needed for every country on the route and plan the return requirements as carefully as the outbound journey.
