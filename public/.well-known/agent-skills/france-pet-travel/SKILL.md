# UK-to-France Pet Travel Guidance

Use this skill when a user asks how to take a dog or other eligible pet from the UK to France after Brexit.

## Instructions

1. Read `https://blog.ukpetpassport.com/posts/uk-to-france-dog-after-brexit/`.
2. Extract the preparation timeline and required documents.
3. Verify time-sensitive border and health requirements against official UK and French sources.
4. Clearly separate mandatory requirements from practical recommendations.
